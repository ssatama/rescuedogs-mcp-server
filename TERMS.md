# Terms of Use

**Last updated: 23 August 2026**

These terms cover the `rescuedogs-mcp-server` MCP server and its remote
endpoint, currently at
`https://rescuedogs-mcp-production.up.railway.app/mcp`
and in future also at `https://mcp.rescuedogs.me/mcp`.

## What this service is

A read-only search interface over a catalogue of dogs listed as available for
adoption by independent rescue organizations in Europe and the UK. It is free to
use and requires no account.

It is a directory, not a rehoming service. Adoption applications, fees,
home checks, transport, and every other part of the process are handled by the
rescue organization directly. This project is not a party to any adoption, is
not an agent of any rescue, and takes no payment.

## Accuracy

Listings are collected from each organization's public pages and refreshed
periodically, so information here can be out of date. A dog shown as available
may already be reserved or adopted. AI-generated personality profiles are
inferred from listing text and are a summary, not an assessment by a vet or
behaviourist.

**Always confirm details with the rescue organization before acting on them.**
Adopting an animal is a long commitment and deserves a real conversation with
the people who know that animal.

## Acceptable use

Use the service for finding dogs to adopt, and for research or reporting about
rescue and rehoming.

Do not use it to harvest contact details for marketing, to resell or
redistribute the catalogue as your own dataset, to acquire animals for resale,
research, breeding, or any purpose other than adoption as a companion, or to
overwhelm the service. The public endpoint is rate limited; do not attempt to
work around those limits.

## Rescue organizations

If you run one of the listed organizations and want your listings removed,
changed, or credited differently, see the removal process in the
[README](README.md#how-the-data-is-gathered-and-how-to-be-removed). Requests are
honoured without justification.
